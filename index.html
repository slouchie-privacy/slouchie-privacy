Privacy Policy for Slouchie

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Slouchie - Posture Tracker ("Slouchie," "we," "us," or "our") is designed to help you develop posture-awareness habits while keeping your posture data on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the app, how it is handled, and how to contact us with privacy questions.

1. Camera Data & On-Device Processing

Slouchie uses your device's camera to perform real-time posture checks. Camera frames are processed on your device to evaluate body positioning for this purpose.

Camera images and video frames are not recorded, stored on our servers, or transmitted by Slouchie. Posture checks are processed on your device.

Slouchie uses camera access for its posture-checking feature. You can manage or revoke camera permission through your device's settings. If you deny camera access, camera-based posture checks will not be available.

2. Data Collection & Local Storage

Slouchie does not require you to create an account or register to use its core features. Your posture calibration settings, completed checks, streak history, progress, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Slouchie does not operate its own server to collect or store this posture history. We do not sell your personal information or use your camera feed for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services & In-App Purchases

Slouchie uses Apple to process in-app purchases and RevenueCat to help manage subscriptions and subscription entitlements. These services may process information needed to complete purchases and determine whether a subscription is active.

  • Apple: Apple processes App Store purchases and handles payment information according to its own privacy practices. Slouchie does not receive your payment card number through the app. Apple's Privacy Policy .
  • RevenueCat: RevenueCat may process purchase history, transaction or receipt information, an app-user identifier, and relevant technical information, depending on the integration. This information is used to manage and verify subscription entitlements. RevenueCat's Privacy Policy .

Slouchie does not send camera images, video frames, or local posture history to RevenueCat as part of its subscription integration. Third-party services handle information under their respective privacy practices.

4. Usage Data & Diagnostics

Slouchie does not include its own advertising or analytics system unless otherwise disclosed. Apple may collect diagnostic information according to your device settings and Apple's privacy practices. Where Apple provides diagnostic information to developers, this depends on the applicable sharing settings.

Slouchie does not use camera footage to identify you or personalize advertising.

5. Children's Privacy

Slouchie does not require users to provide personal information to use its core posture features. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe personal information has been provided to us in connection with Slouchie, please contact us using the email address below so we can review the matter.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Posture data and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app and its associated data, subject to your device's storage and backup behavior.

Subscription and transaction records may be retained by Apple or RevenueCat according to their respective policies and applicable requirements. For privacy requests concerning information we control, contact us by email.

7. Data Security

Keeping posture information on your device reduces the need to transmit it to our systems. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You should keep your device and its operating system updated and use the security features available on your device.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may give you rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held about you. You may also have rights to withdraw consent in applicable circumstances.

To ask a privacy question or submit a request concerning information we control, contact us at the email address below. Requests concerning information held by Apple or RevenueCat may need to be directed to those providers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our app, services, or privacy practices change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
